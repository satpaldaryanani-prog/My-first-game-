# Cat Character Design Specification

## Art Style: Mofusland-Inspired

### Visual Aesthetic
- **Head-to-Body Ratio**: 1:1 or larger (oversized heads)
- **Eye Style**: Large, round, expressive eyes with sparkles
- **Body Shape**: Round, fluffy, chibi proportions
- **Fur Texture**: Soft, cloud-like appearance
- **Accessories**: Cute sports gear (headbands, wristbands, tiny jerseys)

## Character Stats System

### Primary Attributes (Cat-Themed)

1. **Pounce Power** (Shooting Strength)
   - Range: 1-100
   - Determines shot power and long-distance kicks
   - Visual: Larger paw impact effects

2. **Whisker Precision** (Passing Accuracy)
   - Range: 1-100
   - Affects pass accuracy and trajectory control
   - Visual: Glowing whiskers during precise passes

3. **Tail Balance** (Dribbling/Agility)
   - Range: 1-100
   - Controls movement speed and ball control
   - Visual: Animated tail swishing for balance

4. **Claw Grip** (Ball Control)
   - Range: 1-100
   - Determines how well cat holds onto the ball
   - Visual: Paw prints on ball during possession

5. **Nine Lives** (Stamina/Recovery)
   - Range: 1-100
   - Affects energy depletion and recovery rate
   - Visual: Life counter indicator (hearts/paw prints)

6. **Purr-suasion** (Crowd Effect Bonus)
   - Range: 1-100
   - Enhances "Meowmentum" chain system
   - Visual: Sound waves emanating from character

### Secondary Attributes (Personality-Based)

1. **Playfulness**: 1-5 stars
   - High = more likely to chase ball unpredictably
   - Adds character but can be risky

2. **Laziness**: 1-5 stars
   - High = slower sprint speed but better stamina conservation
   - Strategic for long matches

3. **Competitiveness**: 1-5 stars
   - High = aggressive tackles, more fouls
   - Good for defenders

4. **Scaredy-Cat Level**: 1-5 stars
   - High = avoids contact, better at positioning
   - Good for finesse players

## Character Rarity System

### Common (Grey Border)
- **Base Stats**: 40-60 range
- **Evolution Tiers**: 2 (Kitten → Adult)
- **Special Ability**: 1 basic ability
- **Drop Rate**: 60%

### Rare (Blue Border)
- **Base Stats**: 50-70 range
- **Evolution Tiers**: 3 (Kitten → Adult → Champion)
- **Special Abilities**: 2 abilities
- **Drop Rate**: 30%

### Epic (Purple Border)
- **Base Stats**: 60-80 range
- **Evolution Tiers**: 3 (Kitten → Adult → Champion)
- **Special Abilities**: 3 abilities (1 unique)
- **Drop Rate**: 9%

### Legendary (Gold Border)
- **Base Stats**: 70-95 range
- **Evolution Tiers**: 4 (Kitten → Adult → Champion → Legend)
- **Special Abilities**: 4 abilities (2 unique)
- **Unique Traits**: Custom animations, exclusive abilities
- **Drop Rate**: 1%

## Special Abilities System

### Basic Abilities (All Rarities)

1. **Pounce Strike**
   - Quick dash to ball
   - Cooldown: 15 seconds
   - Visual: Speed lines, determined expression

2. **Catnip Boost**
   - 20% speed boost for 5 seconds
   - Cooldown: 30 seconds
   - Visual: Green sparkles, excited animation

3. **Meow Taunt**
   - Distracts nearby opponents
   - Cooldown: 20 seconds
   - Visual: Sound waves, opponents turn heads

### Advanced Abilities (Rare+)

4. **Yarn Ball Curve**
   - Curved shot with heavy spin
   - Cooldown: 25 seconds
   - Visual: Yarn trail behind ball

5. **Furry Fury**
   - Power shot with knockback
   - Cooldown: 40 seconds
   - Visual: Fur stands on end, electrical effect

6. **Shadow Step**
   - Quick dodge move
   - Cooldown: 18 seconds
   - Visual: Brief afterimage trail

### Legendary Abilities (Legendary Only)

7. **Cat Burglar**
   - Steals ball from opponent without tackle
   - Cooldown: 45 seconds
   - Visual: Sneaky, cartoonish steal animation

8. **Catastrophe Kick**
   - Ultra-powerful shot (90% goal chance if on target)
   - Cooldown: 60 seconds
   - Visual: Dramatic slow-mo, screen shake

9. **Royal Decree**
   - Buffs entire team for 10 seconds
   - Cooldown: 90 seconds
   - Visual: Crown appears, team glows gold

## Evolution System

### Evolution Path Example: Orange Tabby "Blaze"

**Tier 1: Kitten Blaze**
- Level: 1-10
- Stats: Base values
- Appearance: Small, playful kitten
- Ability: Pounce Strike only

**Tier 2: Adult Blaze**
- Level: 10-25
- Stats: +20% all stats
- Appearance: Larger, athletic cat with sports headband
- Ability: Pounce Strike + Fireball Shot
- Unlock Cost: 1,000 Fish Coins + 5 Cat Treats

**Tier 3: Champion Blaze**
- Level: 25-40
- Stats: +50% all stats from base
- Appearance: Muscular, confident, jersey with #1
- Ability: All previous + Catnip Boost (enhanced)
- Unlock Cost: 5,000 Fish Coins + 20 Cat Treats + 1 Evolution Stone

**Tier 4: Legend Blaze** (Legendary Only)
- Level: 40-50
- Stats: +100% all stats from base
- Appearance: Glowing effects, championship medal, cape
- Ability: All previous + Catastrophe Kick
- Unlock Cost: 20,000 Fish Coins + 50 Cat Treats + 5 Evolution Stones + 1 Legend Crown

## Character Balancing Philosophy

### Design Principles

1. **Rarity ≠ Power**
   - Legendary cats are unique, not necessarily stronger
   - Common cats can be competitive when fully evolved
   - Player skill > character stats

2. **Rock-Paper-Scissors Balance**
   - Speed beats Power (can dodge)
   - Power beats Defense (breaks through)
   - Defense beats Speed (positioning counters)

3. **Monthly Meta Shifts**
   - Buff underused cats
   - Nerf overused cats (gently)
   - Introduce counters to dominant strategies

4. **Ban List for Tournaments**
   - Top 5 most-used cats banned in competitive
   - Rotates monthly
   - Encourages diverse team building

## Technical Specifications

### Asset Requirements

- **Sprite Resolution**: 2048x2048 maximum
- **Animation Frame Rate**: 12-24 FPS (sprite animation)
- **Color Palette**: Vibrant, saturated colors
- **Art Style**: Clean lines, minimal shading (flat/cel-shaded)
- **File Format**: PNG with alpha channel

### 3D Model Requirements (If Using 3D)

- **Polygon Count**: <2,000 triangles per character
- **Rigging**: Simple skeleton (15-20 bones)
- **Texture Resolution**: 1024x1024
- **Shading**: Toon/cel-shaded for cute aesthetic
- **LOD Levels**: 2 (full detail, simplified distant)
