## 🕹️ Touch Controls

### **Virtual Joystick (Bottom Left)**
- Drag in any direction to move your cat
- Returns to center when released
- The further you drag, the faster you move

### **Sprint Button (Bottom Right - ⚡)**
- **Hold to sprint** (makes you faster)
- **Drains stamina** - watch the green bar!
- Release to regenerate stamina

### **Action Buttons (Bottom Center)**
- **⚽ Button** - Shoot/Pass (normal power)
- **💥 Button** - Power Kick (stronger shot)
- Tap when you have the ball

---

## 📊 On-Screen HUD

### **Top Left (Black Box)**
- **Chain:** Your Meowmentum chain count
- **Bonus:** Current speed boost percentage

### **Top Right (Purple Bar)**
- **Meowmentum Level** (0-5)
- Progress bar shows your chain

### **Right Side (Green Bar)**
- **Stamina meter** (vertical)
- Drains during sprint
- Regenerates when not sprinting

### **Top Header**
- **Score** (left)
- **Time** (right)

## 🎮 How to Play

### **Objective:**
Score more goals than the AI before the 3-minute timer runs out!

### **Your Goal:** Left side (white rectangle)
### **AI Goal:** Right side (white rectangle)

### **Basic Strategy:**

1. **Move** with the joystick to chase the ball
2. **Get the ball** by running into it
3. **Aim** toward the right goal
4. **Shoot** with ⚽ or 💥 button
5. **Sprint** when needed (but watch stamina!)
6. **Build chains** for speed boosts!

---

## ⚡ Meowmentum System (Your Secret Weapon!)

Build your chain to get FASTER:

| Level | Chains Needed | Speed Boost | Visual Effect |
|-------|---------------|-------------|---------------|
| 1 | 3 | +5% | Blue glow |

### **How to Build Chains:**

**Your chain RESETS if AI steals the ball!**

## 💪 Stamina Management

- **Sprinting drains** stamina at 15% per second
- **Regenerates** at 10% per second when not sprinting
- **Can't sprint** when stamina is 0%
- **Smart players** sprint strategically!

**Pro Tip:** Don't hold sprint all the time - save it for when you really need speed!

## 📱 iPhone-Specific Tips

### **Best Experience:**
1. **Play in Safari** (best performance)
2. **Landscape mode** (rotate your phone)
3. **Add to Home Screen** for full-screen play
4. **Turn off Auto-Lock** (Settings → Display → Auto-Lock)
5. **Close other apps** for smooth 60 FPS

### **Add to Home Screen:**
1. Open `mobile.html` in Safari
2. Tap the Share button (square with arrow)
3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
4. Tap "Add"
5. Now you have a game icon on your home screen!

### **Troubleshooting:**
- **Laggy?** Close other apps
- **Controls not working?** Make sure you're touching inside the control areas
- **Can't sprint?** Check your stamina bar (green vertical bar)

## 🎯 Gameplay Tips

1. **Don't chase constantly** - Position yourself between AI and your goal
2. **Build chains early** - Speed boost compounds over time
3. **Sprint to intercept** - Save sprint for getting to loose balls
4. **Aim your shots** - Wait for clear angle before shooting
5. **Use power kicks** - When you have space and time
6. **Defend first** - Keep AI from scoring before attacking
