## 🕹️ Touch Controls (Super Simple!)

### **Left Joystick** 🕹️
Drag to move your cat - the virtual stick will follow your finger

### **Right Button ⚡**
Hold to sprint (watch your green stamina bar!)

### **Bottom Buttons**
- **⚽** = Shoot ball (normal)
- **💥** = Power kick (strong)

---

## 🎯 How to Win

1. **Move** to the ball using the joystick
2. **Get possession** (run into the ball)
3. **Shoot** toward the right goal (white rectangle)
4. **Score more than AI** before time runs out!

---

## ⚡ Pro Tips

- **Build Meowmentum chains** = Get faster! (watch top-right bar)
- **Don't sprint constantly** = Manage your stamina (green bar)
- **Aim carefully** = Wait for clear shot angle
- **Block AI** = Position between AI and your goal

## 🌐 EVEN EASIER: GitHub Pages

If you want to play without downloading:

1. Make sure code is pushed to GitHub:
   ```bash
   cd /home/user/My-first-game-
   git push origin claude/cat-football-game-p6Nys
   ```

2. Enable GitHub Pages:
   - Go to: `github.com/YOUR-USERNAME/My-first-game-`
   - Click **Settings** → **Pages**
   - Under "Build and deployment":
     - Source: "Deploy from a branch"
     - Branch: `claude/cat-football-game-p6Nys`
     - Folder: `/ (root)`
   - Click **Save**

3. Wait 2-3 minutes, then visit:
   ```
   https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/My-first-game-/demo/mobile.html
   ```

4. On your iPhone, open Safari and go to that URL!

5. **Bookmark or Add to Home Screen** for instant access anytime!
